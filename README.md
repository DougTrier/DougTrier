## About me

I spent 33 years doing every job on the plant floor. Now I build the software I wish I'd had:
tools that keep working when the network doesn't, keep your data on your own machines, and
stay out of the way of the person using them.

## How I build

- **Local first.** It works offline, and your data stays on your machines.
- **No lock-in.** No subscriptions and no mandatory cloud.
- **Show the work.** Open source, published test results, checksums and unedited demos.
- **Built for the person using it.** If an operator on night shift can't figure it out, it isn't done.
